About the role
We are seeking an AI and Automation professional who can build practical, business-facing intelligent systems that combine analytics, workflow automation, and applied machine learning. This role sits at the intersection of data, operations, and AI product execution.

Key responsibilities
- Translate ambiguous business questions into scalable analytical workflows and decision-support systems.
- Build automation that reduces manual operational work and improves response speed, monitoring, and reporting.
- Develop AI-powered assistants, agents, or copilots that turn natural-language input into actionable outputs such as analysis, summaries, recommendations, or workflow steps.
- Use Python, SQL, and data tools to transform large datasets into production-ready insights.
- Design dashboards, reports, and visualizations to communicate performance trends, anomalies, and business opportunities.
- Collaborate with operations, product, and business stakeholders to deploy solutions that improve efficiency and adoption.
- Evaluate and integrate AI/ML/NLP techniques where they create measurable value.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with workflow automation, orchestration, or internal tooling.
- Experience with LLM applications, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, or agentic systems.
- Strong Python and SQL skills, with hands-on use of Pandas or similar analytical tooling.
- Experience building analytics products, dashboards, experimentation workflows, or business intelligence assets.
- Familiarity with OCR, NLP, classification, recommendation systems, or applied ML.
- Ability to communicate technical findings clearly and tie them to business impact.

What success looks like
- Delivering automation that meaningfully reduces manual effort and turnaround time.
- Shipping AI-enabled systems that improve business decision-making and user productivity.
- Building reusable analytical workflows that connect data extraction, modeling, visualization, and narrative insight generation.
